Client project · Android app · 2026

PharmaDash: Pharmacy Delivery App

One Flutter app for the pharmacy's managers and its delivery drivers

Android release Flutter · Firebase Admin + Driver roles

Managers see every driver live on a Google Map, create delivery orders and assign them to a driver. Drivers get the order on their phone, tap Start Delivery when they leave the branch, and Mark as Delivered at the door.

A delivery timer starts when the driver leaves. Drivers who arrive within the target time (25 minutes in Sheikh Zayed, 40 in 6th of October) earn a bonus, and managers get a notification at every step. The app runs on Firebase, with sign-in, a live Firestore database and security rules, and works in Arabic and English.
